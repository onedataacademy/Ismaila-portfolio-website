Profile

At the intersection of public health, population science, and statistics.

My work is grounded in a simple idea: strong health decisions begin with evidence that is carefully collected, rigorously analysed, and communicated in a way people can use.

My training spans Health Promotion and Public Health, Demography and Social Statistics, and Statistics. Across academic, research, teaching, community, and applied analytical settings, I have worked with survey data, statistical modelling, research reporting, data visualisation, and public-health problem solving.

I am especially interested in epidemiology, health inequalities, maternal and child health, mental health, sexual and reproductive health, and broader population-health questions that benefit from quantitative inquiry.
